# Band Boosters Platform

## Run the program. Rally the community. Keep the music moving.

**One connected, mobile-first platform for the families, volunteers, directors,
and board members who power a school music program.**

[Request a walkthrough]({{DEMO_URL}}) · [Talk with us](mailto:{{CONTACT_EMAIL}})

Band programs create extraordinary experiences—but running them often means
juggling calendars, signup tools, spreadsheets, forms, payment reports, email
threads, and years of knowledge stored in individual volunteers' heads.

The **Band Boosters Platform** brings that work together. It connects your public
website, family portal, day-to-day operations, nonprofit finances,
communications, and board governance in one role-aware system designed around
the way booster organizations actually work.

## Everything your program needs, working together

### One home for every family

Give parents and students a clear, personal view of what matters: calendars,
event details, volunteer opportunities, forms and documents, balances and
payments, announcements, trips, and varsity letter-point progress. Families can
find answers and take action without hunting through old emails or asking which
system has the latest information.

### Confident operations—from rehearsal to road trip

Run volunteer signups, waitlists, attendance, student rosters, uniforms,
instrument loans, inventory, recognition, background-check status, and trip
logistics from connected records. Mobile-first and offline-capable workflows
support attendance takers, fitting teams, and chaperones where the work actually
happens—not just at a desk.

### Finance built for nonprofit accountability

Track family charges, payment plans, provider payments, invoices,
reimbursements, budgets, fundraising allocations, and ledger activity in one
place. Audit-friendly history and exports help treasurers explain what happened
and prepare board-ready reports. Payment providers such as Zeffy continue to
move the money; the platform remains the operational system of record.

### Events that connect the whole organization

Manage the master calendar, recurring activities, public event pages,
registrations, participant questions, invoices, schedules, checklists,
volunteer needs, reminders, attendance, and follow-up as one lifecycle. From a
weekly rehearsal to a hosted festival, every team works from the same event and
the same season.

### Communication with the right context

Publish news and announcements, send targeted messages and newsletters, schedule
event reminders, and support social-media workflows without rebuilding an
audience list every time. Role- and group-aware targeting helps the right message
reach the right families, students, volunteers, or leaders.

### Governance that survives leadership turnover

Prepare agendas, capture attendance and minutes, record motions and votes, track
action items, publish approved records, and preserve governing documents. Role
handbooks, recurring checklists, handoff packages, and guided annual rollover
turn institutional knowledge into an organization asset—not a departing
volunteer's private archive.

## Designed for everyone who keeps the program moving

- **Families and students** get a simple self-service portal for their schedule,
  commitments, forms, balances, and progress.
- **Coordinators and volunteers** get focused tools for the events, ensembles,
  sections, trips, or responsibilities they lead.
- **Directors and staff** get reliable calendars, rosters, attendance, and
  reports without taking over booster administration.
- **Board officers and administrators** get finance, governance, approvals,
  permissions, continuity, and organization-wide visibility.
- **Community members and visiting schools** get polished public information,
  event registration, news, and support opportunities—without needing an
  internal account.

## Why Band Boosters Platform

- **Purpose-built for booster organizations.** The workflows reflect school
  years, ensembles, family relationships, volunteer leadership, hosted events,
  nonprofit reporting, and board governance.
- **Connected by design.** Calendar events can inform volunteers, attendance,
  points, reminders, registrations, and reports—reducing duplicate entry and
  conflicting versions.
- **Responsibility-based access.** Families see their own information;
  coordinators see the areas they lead; officers and administrators receive the
  broader access their duties require.
- **Mobile where it matters.** Responsive, touch-friendly workflows support
  parents in the stands, volunteers at check-in, and chaperones on the road.
- **Continuity is a feature.** Seasons, role terms, checklists, handbooks,
  handoffs, and archives help each incoming team start ahead instead of starting
  over.
- **Works with familiar services.** Integrations can preserve tools that already
  serve the organization well—including Firebase, Google Calendar and Drive,
  Zeffy, Slack, and Safe Visitor—while the platform becomes the shared source of
  operational truth.
